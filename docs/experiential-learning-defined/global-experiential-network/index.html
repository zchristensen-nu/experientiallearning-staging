One global experience can lead to another

Our worldwide network of partners, campuses, and alumni unlocks boundless global experiential learning opportunities that enrich your education and advance your career.

Benefit from the power of place

Global experiences at our campuses and other locations across the world provide rich opportunities to work, learn, and live in new contexts. Each destination offers its own unique advantages and experiences, and ignites endless possibilities:

  • Build a global mindset and enhanced perspective by studying, working, and collaborating in diverse environments

  • Take part in opportunities that span—and positively impact—the world

  • Develop relationships and expand your professional network that cross industries, borders, and cultures

Live, learn, and innovate without boundaries

A Northeastern education is deeply rooted in real-world learning experiences through a global lens. As a student, you’ll find opportunities to collaborate, earn experience, experiment with AI, and solve problems throughout our global experiential network of campuses across the U.S., U.K., and Canada.

Massachusetts

Our base for national security and defense research, a magnet for science-based startups, and the headquarters for the Kostas Research Institute for Homeland Security. Where innovation is accelerated through flexible R&D partnerships across industry, government, and academia.

Nahant (on another site)

Massachusetts

Home to our Coastal Sustainability Institute. Where cutting-edge research builds resilience among coastal communities facing the effects of climate change.

Portland (on another site)

Maine

Houses the Roux Institute and Institute for Experiential AI, and an engine for economic impact in northern New England. Graduate learning, research collaborations, and innovations drive impact in the life sciences, AI, and computational medicine.
